FAQ

Questions advocates ask

Who is Case Diary for?

Advocates, juniors, and chambers in Pakistan who need a reliable digital case diary for hearings and cause lists.

When do cause list reminders arrive?

Nightly between 8:00 PM and 10:59 PM Asia/Karachi, covering the next day’s hearings. Tap the notification to open Tomorrow Cases.

Can my junior use the same diary?

Yes. Owners can invite subordinates with controlled permissions for viewing, editing, fees, clients, and documents.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Cached cases stay available without internet; changes sync when you reconnect.

Can I track fees and share cause lists?

Record total fee, payments, and balances per matter. Share today’s or this week’s hearings as PDF or image from Home.

Is my data private?

Your account and case data are handled per our Privacy Policy. Contact info@devorium.com for access or deletion requests.

Where can I get help?

Email info@devorium.com or visit our support page.
